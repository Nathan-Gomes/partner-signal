Transparent scoring

Why a partner appears in the queue.

PartnerSignal uses stated discovery signals, not a black-box prediction. Each route is scored from documented needs such as legacy infrastructure, security gaps, AI interest, training needs, and engagement.

  1. Capture the facts: conversation notes and partner context.
  2. Suggest a path: surface the highest-fit service area and specialist.
  3. Keep a human accountable: use the score to prioritize a discovery conversation, then qualify together.

This demonstration uses fictional companies and is not connected to Ingram Micro, Xvantage, or a live CRM.
